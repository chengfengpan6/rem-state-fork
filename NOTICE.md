It does not purport to license proprietary model weights or services,
third-party dependencies, cited publications, private correspondence,

preserved as research records for inspection; the software license is not a
warranty of exclusive rights in those outputs or a license to the model.

The task data are synthetic. Records describe the actually requested model,
access route, and limits on returned version metadata. They do not confer
access to an account or a hosted service. No credentials are included.

Original live records remain unmodified. Any anonymous review export is a
separate local artifact with its own manifest and stated omissions. Citation
credit and applicable third-party notices must be retained.
