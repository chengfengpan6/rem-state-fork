# Bounded v2 engineering verification, 25 October 2026

This plan is written before selecting the history or dispatching new responses.
It operationalizes the user's ceiling of **at most 20 model calls per branch**,
not 20 rounds or 20 independent histories. It is not a preregistered scientific
study. The earlier one-history demonstration informed the protocol changes.

## Purpose and scope

Check delivery of P/E/H/R0/RU, separation of recall and own estimates, stage-only
map instructions, limited interleaved branch scheduling, readout aggregation,
attempt accounting and the exact-replay/semantic verifier. The statistical
object remains an organizationally relevant shared interpretation above a
common factual record. Engineering success cannot establish an organizational
mechanism or human external validity.

## Formation-only history eligibility

Enumerate integers starting20261025, ascending, with a maximum of100 candidates.
Use the DGP's first three formation observations only. Compute E0 from the
Beta(1,1) model. P swaps the complete estimate/lower/upper cells corresponding
to the true best and worst sources in each context. Display both to two decimals.
Accept the first candidate whose rendered P and E differ and for which the
unweighted nine-cell squared-error mean relative to the true reliability matrix
is larger for P. Save every candidate examined and the mechanical diagnostics.

Do not inspect actual model-generated H, future outcome scores, or model outputs
to select a seed. No future-tape criterion is applied. This selected diagnostic
history does not estimate a natural history frequency; a larger map error does
not guarantee larger future cBR. The single R0 replicate supplies one descriptive
difference, not a noise distribution, significance threshold or power estimate.

## Acceptance and reporting

Before live: meaningful offline invariants and fault injections pass; old v1
evidence strictly replays; configured cost is checked against all caps; source
fingerprint and final config are saved. After live: preserve every outcome and
failure, verify intended payload semantics, compare scored artifacts after exact
replay, report requested/returned identity and logical/physical/replayed counts.
No substantive result is selected on sign. An unsuccessful call remains a failure
even if explicit budgeted recovery subsequently completes the logical sequence.

Formal research remains a separate protocol: candidate24+84, U12, multiple
independent histories, justified N and predefined inferential windows. Neither
the U1 engineering schedule nor a single diagnostic seed supplies that evidence.
